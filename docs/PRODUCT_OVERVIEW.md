# Dramora — Product Overview

## Product identity

- **Name:** Dramora
- **Tagline:** Where Stories Become Productions.
- **Publisher:** Brilina Dev
- **Product category:** AI-assisted video production workspace
- **Scope:** Multiple forms of video production, not limited to micro-drama.

## Product vision

Dramora brings the planning and creative coordination of video production into one workspace. It helps creators develop ideas, organize production knowledge, prepare assets and prompts, coordinate external AI-generation workflows, and track the progress of a production.

Dramora is not intended to be only a chat interface or a single-purpose video generator.

## Core product capabilities

1. **AI Showrunner** — central conversational interface for understanding requests and executing authorized production actions.
2. **Projects and productions** — organize work while supporting different production formats.
3. **Production Bible** — maintain structured creative context and continuity.
4. **Creative references** — manage characters, locations, wardrobe, props, visual direction, and related references.
5. **Production planning** — organize productions into the structures they need, such as scenes, shots, clips, episodes, or seasons.
6. **Asset management** — organize generated and uploaded assets, versions, approvals, and relationships.
7. **AI provider integrations** — connect text and image capabilities through provider adapters.
8. **External video workflows** — prepare prompts and track externally generated clips without assuming a built-in video-generation engine or editor.
9. **Exports** — provide useful individual downloads and complete production exports.
10. **Settings and identity** — support account preferences and project-specific configuration.

## Product principles

- Structured records are the canonical source of truth.
- A production does not have to contain seasons or episodes.
- Users should not need a local GPU or local AI model.
- Provider integrations should be replaceable where practical.
- Background tasks need observable status, safe retries, and clear failure states.
- Approved assets and destructive operations need appropriate protection.
- The experience should work across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Not assumed for the initial release

- An in-house video-generation model.
- A built-in nonlinear video editor or automatic clip-stitching engine.
- A user-facing multi-agent control panel.
- A subscription or daily AI-credit system.
- An administrative dashboard.

These boundaries can change only through an explicit product decision.
